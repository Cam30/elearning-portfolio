The bridge inspection

Situation. Your drone is inspecting a bridge above an active maintenance crew. The camera captures identifiable workers, vehicle registrations and a contractor tablet displaying project information. The flight team did not give workers a capture notice.

Decision 1. What is your first operational response?

Decision 2. The footage has already synced to a cloud inspection platform. What now?

Shared field principles

Minimise
Capture only what the inspection needs. Reframe, mask or restrict unnecessary detail.
Notify
Plan worker and contractor notice before operating over an active worksite.
Control
Define access, storage, third-party use, retention and deletion before upload.

Important. Workplace surveillance and privacy obligations vary by jurisdiction and context. Escalate uncertainty. Do not treat project authorisation as blanket privacy consent.
