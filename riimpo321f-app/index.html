Site 7: Northlink Subdivision

You've been rostered onto a live civil construction site. Six shifts stand between you and operator sign-off. Every shift mixes briefing, decision-making and hands-on judgement — the same calls a real loader operator makes before the key turns.

🪧 Your site induction

The job: Northlink is a 42-lot residential subdivision. Bulk earthworks are done; you're on stockpile management, truck loading, trimming to design level and drainage-trench backfill. Your plant is a 3.5 m³ articulated wheeled front end loader.

The rules: You learn by doing. Every activity gives you an immediate call — right, wrong, or partly there — and tells you why. Getting it wrong first go costs you XP, not your job. Retry as many times as you need.

The goal: Clear all six shifts, bank 8 competency badges, and reach Leading Hand rank.

⚡ Earn XPFull points for a first-attempt call. Partial credit if you self-correct. Nothing is locked out by failure.
